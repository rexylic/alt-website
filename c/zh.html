A word-initial consonantal i or u is written j or v (e.g. jeshou, vansuei). If a syllable-initial i and u does not follow a space, substitute them with j or ji, v or vu (e.g. ziji, cijou, jauvan). For other syllable-inital vowels that do not follow a space, prepend an apostrophe (e.g. si'an).

The only non-ASCII symbol used is ı, which usage (I think) is perfectly justified. It's a wonderful letter. Optionally, you may substitute letter runs with the following:

ŋ for ng č for ch š for sh ž for zh
é for ei ó for ou y for iu w for uv

y acts both like a consonant and a vowel; it should never follow an apostrophe.

Here's a sample passage with Pinyin, the same passage written in my system, and the same passage with all substitutions.

renren sheng'erziyou, zai zunyan he quanli shang yilü pingdeng. tamen fuyou lixing he liangxin, bing ying yi xiongdi guanxi de jingshen xiang duidai. renren you zige xiangyou ben xuanyan suozaide yiqie quanli he ziyou, bufen zhongzu, fuse, xingbie, yuyan, zongjiao huo qita jianjie, guoji huo shehui chushen, caichan, chusheng huo qita shenfen deng renhe qubie. bingqie bude yin yiren suoshude guojia huo lingtu de zhengzhide, xingzhengde huozhe guojide diwei zhi butong er yousuo qubie, wulun gai lingtu shi duli lingtu, tuoguan lingtu, feizizhi lingtu huozhe chuyu qita renhe zhuquan shouxianzhide qingkuang zhixia.
